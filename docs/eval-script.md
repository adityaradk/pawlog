# PawLog – Heuristic Evaluation Script (Nov 17)

**Prototype:** https://adityaradk.github.io/pawlog/ — click **Continue with demo account**.
Between evaluators: account menu (top right) → **Reset demo data**.

## Pitch (read aloud)

PawLog is a pet health tracker for first-time cat and dog owners. You record vet visits, vaccinations, medications and weights, and PawLog turns them into a clear list of what is overdue, due today, and coming up next.

## Target users

- **Primary:** first-time cat and dog owners managing routine care (vaccines, checkups, short medication courses).
- **Secondary:** experienced owners and people with more than one pet.

## Tasks for evaluators

Read each task as written. Do not point at buttons or name screens.

1. **Miso just had a vet visit today. Record it, including that the vet wants an ear recheck in 3 weeks.**
2. **Miso's ear drops are due. Record that you gave them. Then find a dose you didn't give and confirm it wasn't recorded as given.**
3. **Find Miso's previous weight and how much it changed.**

If time remains: free exploration on a phone-width window.

## Heuristics we most want feedback on

1. **Visibility of system status** – Is it always clear what was saved, what is due, and what state a dose is in?
2. **Error prevention** – Do the forms and confirmations stop mistakes (wrong dates, wrong units, accidental deletes, “dismissed” vs. “given”)?
3. **Recognition rather than recall** – Can you find past records, linked follow-ups and previous weights without remembering where they are?

Feedback on any of Nielsen’s other 7 heuristics is welcome too.

## Moderator notes

- **Do not help the evaluator.** If they ask “where is…?”, reply “What would you try?” and note where they got stuck.
- Ask them to **think aloud** the whole time (“Tell us what you’re looking for and what you expect to happen”).
- One teammate moderates, one takes notes directly in [feedback-log.md](feedback-log.md) (screen, issue, heuristic, severity).
- After each task, ask: “On a scale of 1–5, how confident are you that it worked?”
- Reset demo data before the next team starts.
